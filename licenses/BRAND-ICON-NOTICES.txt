Official application and website identification icons

Bundled locally in frontend/assets/brands/ for the application access page.
Sources, acquisition dates and hashes are recorded beside the assets in
website-sources.json, application-sources.json and codex-source.json.

The marks identify third-party applications and websites. Trademark and
copyright rights remain with their respective owners. Their inclusion does
not imply sponsorship, affiliation or endorsement of MC Proxy.

The original downloaded icon artwork is preserved. Local CSS controls only
its display size and surrounding background for contrast. No icon request
is sent to an external website when the application access page is opened.
